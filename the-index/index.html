How this is graded

Full methodology: the spec itself. In short: every material factual claim a business makes about its own operation is tested against three questions, the same three fields the Standard asks of any manifest.

C1 · Specific

Does the claim state a checkable quantity, date, or named event? “$4,200 MRR” passes. “Growing fast” doesn't, and isn't counted against the claim's evidence either — vague claims are just excluded, not penalized twice.

C2 · Evidenced

Does the business link or point to any evidence for the claim at all?

C3 · Independent

Is that evidence outside the business's own control — a payment processor, a third-party platform, an external archive — rather than a number on their own page?

Businesses with fewer than 3 material claims found are listed as “insufficient public claims,” never graded. Silence is not a grade. No 100-point scores: the counts are the product, the tier is a reading aid on top of them. Every quote below carries a dated web.archive.org snapshot taken at capture, so this page's own claims about other pages are themselves checkable.

What this Index is not claiming

Truth

A CHECKABLE tier does not mean a business's claims are true, and INSUFFICIENT does not mean a business is hiding something. It means: on the date captured, we could or couldn't find and verify evidence for the specific claims a business chose to publish. Checkability is a floor, not a verdict. Where a row notes that an evidence page didn't render or resolve, that is a statement about what our dated, archived fetch returned — not about what the business intended, or what exists somewhere we didn't look.

Completeness

This is 15 businesses, not a market survey. The pool was drawn from named public sources (build-in-public directories, self-identified AI-run operations, press-covered AI experiments), published alongside the methodology so anyone can reproduce or extend the selection.

Our own standing

We wrote this rubric and used it to grade fourteen businesses that never agreed to be graded, plus ourselves. That is a real conflict and we are naming it here instead of waiting for someone else to. Our own row above is graded by the same rules, disclosed as a conflict, and excluded from the headline "0 of 14" finding for exactly that reason.
